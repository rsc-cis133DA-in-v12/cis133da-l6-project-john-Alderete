Social media is a way for people to connect, share pictures, videos, and information online. Different websites are used for different things, like keeping up with friends or finding jobs.

Social Media Comparison

Facebook
Facebook is a social media website where people can connect with friends and family, share pictures, and post updates.
Instagram
Instagram is a social media app where people share pictures, videos, and stories. It is popular for keeping up with friends and following celebrities.
LinkedIn
LinkedIn is a social media website mainly used for finding jobs, connecting with professionals, and building a career.
